01 / Keep the objects linear

Points, lines,
and coherent faces.

The Penrose page changes the geometric objects. Here we change the incidence pattern.

Alternate point labels and line labels around each quadrilateral. Adjacent labels must be nonincident. A face labeled A, ℓ, B, m is coherent when the joining line AB contains the point ℓ ∩ m, with the required constructions well-defined.

rf = ℓ(A)m(B) / [m(A)ℓ(B)]
coherence ⇔ rf = 1

The point–line cube demonstrates a Desargues configuration. Drag its perspective center or move one vertex along its perspective ray. Reveal the final line through the three side-intersection points.

02 / Local ratios, global cancellation

The boundary
has no boundary.

On an oriented, bicolored quadrilateral decomposition of a closed surface, each nonzero edge pairing occurs once in a numerator and once in a denominator. Thus the product of all face ratios is one.

∏faces f rf = 1

Coherence on all but one face forces coherence on the last face. In the exact edge-weight experiment, double one edge: its two incident face ratios change reciprocally. Their total product remains exactly one.

Two levels of data. Arbitrary nonzero edge weights need not come from geometric point–line labels. The exact arithmetic demonstration isolates the cancellation argument; it does not claim geometric realizability.

03 / A surface encodes a proof

A different organization
of classical incidence.

Fomin and Pylyavskyy use different labeled surfaces to obtain different incidence theorems. Desargues is encoded on a sphere using a cube; Pappus has a torus encoding. The surface is a combinatorial organizer, not a tiling by rigid metric shapes.

The interactive here is the cube example. A manipulable torus encoding of Pappus is not yet included. The paper also has a separate seven-to-eight completion result; that existence statement is not identical to the all-but-one-face implication on already supplied labels.

04 / The formal boundary

What the library proves.

The Lean surface module proves general finite edge cancellation and the last-face consequence for a pairing certificate. Its two bijections record the occurrence of each edge in the positive and negative slots. The structure does not yet include a topological realization of an arbitrary quadrangulated surface.

The coordinate coherence theorem and the independent classical Desargues statement are also checked. Full assembly of the paper’s geometric surface theorem from arbitrary admissible labels remains a separate task.
