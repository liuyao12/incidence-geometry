01 / An established common language

Matrices and minors.

There is a checked local algebraic connection. A full common geometric theorem has not yet been established.

For a scalar pivot k, the Schur update is M′ij = Mij − MikMkj/Mkk. Two eliminations commute when the required pivots and the 2 × 2 pivot minor are nonzero. Symmetry is preserved.

This is the established M-system studied by Bobenko–Schief, not a new recurrence. Our contribution at this stage is a verified organization of its local algebra and its specializations into the existing formal APIs.

02 / Two specializations

A vanishing minor.
A repeated factor.

Fomin face coherence says a 2 × 2 point–hyperplane pairing matrix is singular. Penrose’s contact formula is a symmetric specialization of a determinantal exchange: the two factors of the defect coincide, making a square.

D(dq − ur) − d det N
= (dv − au)(ds − br)N = [[q,u,v],[r,d,a],[s,b,e]], D = de − ab.

The checked comparison derives an existing Penrose middle-edge identity and an existing Fomin mixed-ratio identity from this shared algebra. Neither deduction proves a reduction between the complete geometric theories.

03 / Do not divide away the incidence

The singular configurations
are part of the subject.

A coherent Fomin face has a zero determinant, despite all four edge pairings being nonzero. A two-pivot elimination that divides by that determinant is unavailable exactly there.

This motivates a division-free language of minor relations, with Schur complements as regular coordinate charts. It is not enough to prove everything on an invertible chart and silently substitute a singular value afterward.

04 / A constraint on possible gluing

Retain the defect.

For a closed pairing certificate, let Δf = afdf − bfcf and nf = bfcf. Then edge cancellation gives

∏f (1 + Δf/nf) = 1.

Factorized defects can be retained, but the assignment is not automatically realizable by a matrix of minors or by a geometric configuration. Over an ordered field, setting every Δf = uf² with every nf positive forces all the uf to vanish. A nontrivial unification needs more than this positive-square ansatz.

05 / Research, not a third theorem yet

What would count
as a common generalization?

It should accept arbitrary admissible geometric input on both sides, retain signs and singular minor data, prove compatibility around surface cycles, and recover the appropriate geometric conclusions. Penrose requires completion by a missing conic, not only an identity among already supplied labels.

That translation and global realization are not yet proved. This page will be the home for a new result if one is established; for now it is a map of checked connections and unresolved tasks.
